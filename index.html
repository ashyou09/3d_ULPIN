Rights, restrictions, responsibilities

InterestPartyShare

ISO 19152 LADM. Rights change constantly and never touch the ID or the geometry.

A contractor draws a trench and enters a depth. The engine returns every registered volume the trench could strike, each buffered by twice its own positional uncertainty — so a poorly surveyed line is treated as more dangerous, not less.

Verified — public record

  • Mumbai Metro Line 3 (Aqua) — India's first fully underground corridor: 33.5 km, 27 stations of which 26 are underground. Its BKC station opened 5 October 2024, directly beneath this district.
  • Metro Line 2B is elevated with a planned interchange at Income Tax Office, BKC — one district with both a subsurface and an overhead corridor.
  • BKC high-speed rail station is under construction underground with commercial development intended above it — a live Indian air-rights-over-infrastructure case.
  • NAKSHA launched 18 February 2025 across 152 pilot ULBs in 26 states and 3 UTs.
  • ULPIN / Bhu-Aadhaar, the 14-character parcel ID, is live in 29 states including Maharashtra.
  • MahaRERA publishes approved layout plans, floor plans and carpet-area statements per project.
  • Ground conditions — BKC is reclaimed Mithi floodplain over Deccan trap basalt, which is why Line 3 was bored in rock.
  • FSI is a real statutory control in Indian DCPR, and is genuinely independent of the height limit — the point this model turns on.

Modelled for this demonstration

  • The orthophoto is rendered by code, not photographed — so no licensed imagery is redistributed.
  • Plot dimensions, tower footprint, unit layout and slab elevations are a plausible BKC-scale composite, not a surveyed building.
  • Parent parcel 27MH0BKC0G0114 is illustrative. 27 is Maharashtra's state code; the rest is not NIC-issued.
  • The permissible FSI of 4.00 and the 70 m height limit are indicative for a BKC-type commercial plot, not the sanctioned figures for any specific plot.
  • Tunnel axis depth 20 m and the 6.35 m bore are representative of a deep TBM section, not MMRCL design data for this chainage.
  • Owners, liens, easement parties and strata depths are indicative.

Why this site

  • No other Indian district puts a deep tunnel, an elevated deck, an underground station with air-rights above it and rectilinear, publicly mapped MMRDA plots in one place. Every Z-class has a real referent here.
